A collection of high-performance userscripts designed to enhance your **Travian Legends** and **Northern Legends** experience. From resource management to wave building and ad skipping, these tools automate tedious tasks and provide critical strategic information.

---

## 🚀 Featured Scripts

### 📊 [TravianResourceBarPlus](https://github.com/bourama1/Travian-scripts/raw/main/TravianResourceBarPlus/TravianResourceBarPlus.user.js)
The ultimate resource management dashboard.
- **Real-time Tracking:** Monitor production, capacity, and fill times across all villages.
- **Visual Alerts:** Color-coded warnings for full warehouses or impending starvation.
- **Built-in Tools:** Includes a battle analyzer, crop finder, and construction tips.
- **Enhanced UI:** Adds quick-link icons for barracks, stable, workshop, and marketplace.

### ⏳ [TravianTaskQueue](https://github.com/bourama1/Travian-scripts/raw/main/TravianTaskQueue/TravianTaskQueue.user.js)
Intelligent construction and military scheduler.
- **Smart Queuing:** Schedule delayed constructions, upgrades, and research.
- **Military Automation:** Plan attacks, raids, or reinforcements to trigger at specific times.
- **Village Switching:** Automatically handles village switching to execute tasks.
- **Build-Time Reduction:** Integrates with AdSkipper to click "Watch Video" buttons automatically.

### 🌊 [TravianWaveBuilder](https://github.com/bourama1/Travian-scripts/raw/main/TravianWaveBuilder/TravianWaveBuilder.user.js)
Precision wave attack coordinator.
- **Perfect Timing:** Launch multiple attack waves with millisecond intervals.
- **Dynamic Management:** Add, remove, or reorder waves directly from the rally point.
- **Supports All Tribes:** Custom configurations for Romans, Teutons, Gauls, and more.

### ⏩ [TravianAdSkipper](https://github.com/bourama1/Travian-scripts/raw/main/TravianAdSkipper/TravianAdSkipper.user.js)
Seamless build-time reduction.
- **Auto-Skip:** Bypasses video ads and automatically claims rewards.
- **Global Reach:** Works across all Travian Legends localized servers.
- **Silent Operation:** Runs in the background without interrupting your gameplay.

## 🛠 How to Install

1.  **Install a Userscript Manager:**
    *   **Chrome/Edge/Brave:** [Tampermonkey](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/tampermonkey/dhdgffkkebhmkfjojejmpbldmpobfkfo) or [Violentmonkey](https://violentmonkey.github.io/get-it/).
    *   **Firefox:** [Tampermonkey](https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/tampermonkey/) or [Greasemonkey](https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/greasemonkey/).
    *   **Safari:** [Userscripts Safari Extension](https://apps.apple.com/app/userscripts/id1463298887).
    *   **Android:** Firefox, Kiwi, or Yandex Browser with Tampermonkey/Violentmonkey extensions.

2.  **Click a script link above** to open the installation page.
3.  Click **Install** when prompted by your manager.
